RE:VENTURE

The Problem Changes. Your Strategy Must Too.

Step into the role of a strategic decision-maker for a real company facing a real business challenge. Your team will analyse the situation, identify the core problem, and build an initial strategy.

But the challenge doesn't stay the same.

In the next round, the market shifts. A new competitor, regulation, customer behaviour, supplier issue, technology, or strategic opportunity can change the situation. You must rethink your strategy, make difficult decisions, assess risks, and adapt.

Then comes the Deal Room — a controlled negotiation where you can form partnerships, exchange resources, make alliances, or choose to remain independent.
