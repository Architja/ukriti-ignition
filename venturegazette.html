VENTURE GAZETTE

Today's News. Tomorrow's Venture.

What if a newspaper wasn't just something you read, but a source of your next business idea?

In VENTURE GAZETTE, your team gets a newspaper and a simple challenge: look beyond the headlines.

Find a problem, emerging trend, major development, changing behaviour, or hidden opportunity in the day's news. Then turn that observation into a product or service that people would actually want — and build a business around it.

You aren't just looking for a creative idea. You need to show why the opportunity exists, who needs your solution, how your venture makes money, and why it can grow.

01

FIND THE OPPORTUNITY

Scan the newspaper. Spot a problem, trend, or change. Identify the people affected and turn your observation into a potential business opportunity.

02

BUILD THE VENTURE

Transform your opportunity into a venture. Define the product, customer, value proposition, business model, go-to-market strategy, competitive advantage, and scalability.

03

THE VENTURE PITCH

Present your venture and make the case for why your idea can work in the real world.
